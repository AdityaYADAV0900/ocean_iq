⚓ Port Trust Under-Keel Clearance (UKC) Regulations & Navigation Guidance

Regulatory depth thresholds and tidal draft restrictions enforced across Indian coal terminals.

Port Trust Operational Guidelines
🌊 Paradip Port (Odisha)

Max permissible draft 16.5m (outer channel) and 14.5m (inner berths). Capesize bulkers exceeding 14.5m must discharge at deep CQ-1/CQ-2 berths during high water or perform offshore lighterage.

⚓ Gangavaram Port (AP)

Deepest non-major bulk terminal on India's eastern seaboard with 18.2m water depth. Can accept fully-laden Capesize & Newcastlemax bulkers (up to 200,000 DWT) without lighterage.

🚢 Visakhapatnam (Vizag)

Outer harbor handles up to 14.5m draft (Panamax standard). Inner harbor strictly limited to 10.5m draft with maximum 195m LOA. Capesize requires SPM or outer berths.

⚠️ Haldia Dock Complex (WB)

Riverine port on Hooghly estuary governed by severe tidal draft limits (8.0m to 8.5m). Capesize & Panamax vessels cannot enter fully laden; strictly limited to Handysize or double-banking barge transshipment.
